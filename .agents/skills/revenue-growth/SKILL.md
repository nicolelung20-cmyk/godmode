# Revenue Growth

1. Verify the live product and current conversion path.
2. Identify the highest-value broken or missing link: demand, capture, qualification, offer, checkout, delivery, or measurement.
3. Implement the smallest reversible improvement.
4. Instrument measurable events.
5. Verify real transactions separately from simulated or test activity.
6. Never claim revenue without a confirmed payment event.
